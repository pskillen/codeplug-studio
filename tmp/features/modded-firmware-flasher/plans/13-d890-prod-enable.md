## Context

Each radio's firmware updater carries its own `prodFlashDisabled` flag. While it is `true`, that radio is hidden on
the **prod** build only. A radio may be enabled in prod only when it is **proven**: Studio's updater has flashed both
its **stock** firmware and **at least one patched release** on real hardware.

For the AT-D890UV:

- **H4** (phase 11): release `1.0.0` was flashed from `dev`, and Device Info showed `1.0.0`;
- **H5** (phase 12): stock V1.05 was flashed from `dev`, and Device Info showed "Ver 1.05".

**The orchestrator starts this phase only after the owner has confirmed both.** This tiny PR:

- sets `prodFlashDisabled: false`;
- sets `stockFlashHardwareProven: true`, which removes the "not yet hardware-proven" label from the D890 stock entry;
- updates the docs.

The DM-32UV is untouched; it was enabled in phase 9.
