## Context

Each radio's firmware updater carries its own `prodFlashDisabled` flag. While it is `true`, that radio is hidden on
the **prod** build (`__BUILD_ENV__ === 'prod'`) and fully available on next, dev and staging. A radio may be enabled
in prod only when it is **proven**: Studio's updater has flashed both its **stock** firmware and **at least one of
our patched releases** on real hardware.

For the DM-32UV:

- **H2** (phase 6): stock v1.01.048 was flashed from `dev`;
- **H3** (phase 8): release `1.0.0` was flashed from `dev`.

**The orchestrator starts this phase only after the owner has confirmed both.** This PR is a deliberately tiny,
separately reviewable flip. The AT-D890UV flag is untouched.
