A release candidate for 1.0.0, for testing. It applies to stock firmware **v1.01.048** only. It has the same mods as the 1.0.0 release; the final release is the build that followed it.

Everything not listed here behaves exactly as stock. The codeplug format is unchanged, so the official CPS and Codeplug Studio read and write the radio as before, and the radio keeps its channels and settings across the update.

**What's in it**

- **Zone scan:** pressing Scan on a channel with no scan list scans the whole current zone (up to 64 channels) instead of refusing. While it walks, the screen shows the zone name and `Scanning...`. It resumes about 5 s after a signal ends. Channels with a scan list scan it exactly as stock.
- **Quick menu and light mode:** assign the stock **Flashlight** key function to a free programmable key (SK1, SK2 or P1; avoid P2). It opens a small menu with one row, **Light mode**, which inverts the screen for easier reading in daylight. The choice is saved across power-off.
- **Position report retry:** a timed position report waits for a GPS fix instead of being skipped until the next interval.
- **GPS position padding fix:** Fixed Beacon position reports keep leading zeros in latitude and longitude, so APRS gateways can read them.
- **Text fixes:** eleven English typos and wording fixes in menus and pop-ups.

**Known limits:** nuisance delete during a zone scan is untested, and the zone-scan hang time is fixed at about 5 s.

**Checking it worked:** the radio does not show the mod's version yet. Device information still reads `DM32.01.L01.048`. Check that Scan on a channel with no scan list starts a zone scan.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock v1.01.048 from this page, or use the official CPS.
