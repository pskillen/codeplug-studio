A release candidate for 1.1.0, for testing. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only. It has three of the six planned 1.1.0 features. The others follow in a later candidate.

**Not yet tried on a radio:** the new items below are checked in an emulator only. Everything in 1.0.0 is also in this build.

**What's new since 1.0.0**

- **Mod version:** the power-on screen shows `mm9pdy-1.1.0-rc.1`, and Menu > Settings > Device Info has a new **Mod Ver:** item with the same text. Firmware Ver: still shows the stock version.
- **Channel with no contact:** a digital channel with no transmit contact starts on the first talk group in its RX group list, not on talk-group slot 0. This needs TG Next or TG Previous assigned to a key. A channel with no contact and no RX group list behaves as stock.
- **TG Next / TG Previous during a call:** they work while a call is being received. Main side and digital memory channels only; they do nothing while the sub side is receiving.

**Known limits:** whether the radio stores and reads "no contact" as the emulator does is untested. What the call does after a step during a call is unconfirmed.

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.1.0-rc.1`.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock V1.05 from this page, or use the official CPS.
